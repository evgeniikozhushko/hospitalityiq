# HospitalityIQ starter test questions

Draft v0. Marketing-focused: guest reviews plus a few marketing docs.
Financial documents are out of scope; the system must decline those.

## Review insight
1. What do guests most often complain about at our hotels in winter?
2. What do guests praise most about our restaurants?
3. Which properties get the most mentions of cleanliness, good or bad?
4. How has feedback about check-in changed over the last year?
5. What do families say compared with couples?

## Marketing content
6. Write a short property blurb using what guests love most about [property].
7. What phrases do guests use that we could reuse in ad copy?
8. Which amenities do guests mention that our property descriptions leave out?

## Must decline / say not found
9. What was last quarter's revenue at [property]? (expected: decline, no financial data)
10. What do guests say about a property we have no reviews for? (expected: say no data)
11. Which winter promotion drove the most direct bookings? (expected: campaign intent, "not available yet". This is a missing feature, not a correct refusal)
